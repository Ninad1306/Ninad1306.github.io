Projects

Evaluation Frameworks for Catastrophic Forgetting in LLM Pretraining

Investigated the stability-plasticity dilemma in LLMs, where sequential training causes gradient conflicts that overwrite earlier knowledge. Evaluated degradation with Backward Transfer (BWT) for global retention and MICA for class level accuracy tracking across task transitions, and studied subset selection as a way to balance old knowledge against new tasks.

Rapid KNN

An optimized K nearest neighbors algorithm that uses sampling to cut computational overhead, with PyNNDescent integrated for approximate nearest neighbor indexing. It scales better and has lower complexity than traditional KNN on large, high dimensional datasets.
